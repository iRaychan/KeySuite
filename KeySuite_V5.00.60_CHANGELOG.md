# KeySuite V5.00.60

- Added 37 complete KV 12 and KV 15 catalogue models from the supplied V1.5 workbook, with their motor, dimensional, and hydraulic data. Existing KV, KVC and KVN entries remain available.
- Refreshed KV 12 and KV 15 curve samples from the workbook. An unused impeller power entry marked `x` now contributes zero instead of making shaft power unavailable.
- Replaced the 13 CHC C4 Product and Selection Page 3 drawings using the supplied variant and size map. The original PNG pixels are composited on white at their native dimensions for legible browser printing.
- Kept the V5.00.59 browser Print / Save as PDF workflow and existing page layouts.

Three incomplete KV variants remain withheld: KV 12-20-2, KV 15-10-1 and KV 15-190-1. The workbook contains no new prices for the 37 models; price entries are intentionally unset and the existing manual price workflow remains available.
