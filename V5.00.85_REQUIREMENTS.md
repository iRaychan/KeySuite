# BUG 001 — GLOBAL COMMERCIAL BRAND & MODEL IDENTITY

## Background

This issue has repeatedly appeared across KeySuite versions.

Previous model-specific fixes have not permanently solved it.

**This is a global architectural defect requiring a global repair.**

Do not implement another isolated VMS, VEC, CHC or B.G.Reich patch.

## Universal commercial identity rule

Every product and component must retain its **own selected commercial brand, commercial series and customer-facing model**.

This applies to:

- All current brands and future brands.
- All product series and model families.
- Individual quotations.
- Assembly quotations.
- Complete and partial Pumpset quotations.
- System quotations.
- Manual quotation items with assigned brands.
- BOM and costing descriptions.
- On-screen quotation descriptions.
- Saved and reopened quotations.
- Generated quotation PDFs.

### Brand handling

- B.G.Reich → B.G.Reich.
- VEC → VEC.
- O.K.Pump → O.K.Pump.
- Megaflow → Megaflow.
- TESK → TESK.
- Other brands → their own selected commercial brand.
- Brand `-` → no brand prefix.

Brand `-` is intentional and must not trigger a default B.G.Reich fallback.

### Internal classification versus commercial model

Internal product groupings must never be blindly inserted into customer-facing model names.

Example:

Internal series: CHC C6

Commercial model: CHC 10-4

Correct description:

`B.G.Reich Vertical Multistage Pump Model: CHC 10-4`

Incorrect:

`B.G.Reich Vertical Multistage Pump Model: CHC C6 10-4`

Do not blindly prepend a series prefix if the commercial model already contains the correct identifier.

### Confirmed incorrect examples

**Case A — VEC VMS 150-2-1**

Selected: VEC / VMS / VMS 150-2-1

Incorrect:

`B.G.Reich Vertical Multistage Pump Model: VMS 150-2-1`

Expected:

`VEC Vertical Multistage Pump Model: VMS 150-2-1`

**Case B — Brandless VMS 10-4**

Selected: `-` / VMS / VMS 10-4

Incorrect:

`B.G.Reich Vertical Multistage Pump Model: CHC C6 10-4`

Expected:

`Vertical Multistage Pump Model: VMS 10-4`

**Case C — Brandless VMS 150-2-1**

Selected: `-` / VMS / VMS 150-2-1

Incorrect:

`Vertical Multistage Pump Model: CHC 150-2-1`

Expected:

`Vertical Multistage Pump Model: VMS 150-2-1`

### Required implementation

Trace and repair the commercial identity pipeline:

Product Selection → Product Curve/Product List → Quote Payload → Quotation Item → Assembly/Pumpset/System → Description → Save → Reopen → PDF.

Separate commercial identity from:

- Hydraulic curve source.
- Internal product grouping.
- Pricing source.
- Costing and BOM source.
- Shared technical data.

Engineering data may be reused internally, but it must never overwrite the selected commercial identity.

Use a shared, consistent identity-resolution mechanism rather than scattered brand-specific replacements.

Preserve component-specific identities in mixed-brand assemblies and pumpsets.

Do not change existing pricing, brand margins, BOM calculations or technical performance values unless directly required by the approved BSW update.

# BUG 002 — QUOTATION ENTRY-PATH INCONSISTENCY

## Confirmed investigation findings

Work observed that the same VMS 150-2-1 product produces different results depending on the quotation entry path.

**Product Curve → Quote**

- Retained VMS commercial model.
- Incorrectly inserted B.G.Reich brand.
- Unexpectedly changed the quotation template from Keylargo to TESK.

**Product List → Quote**

- Replaced VMS with CHC in the generated description.
- Omitted the commercial brand.
- Retained Keylargo template.

These are separate observed failures.

## Requirements

1. Audit all quotation entry points, including Product Curve and Product List.
2. Ensure both paths use the same commercial identity rules.
3. Preserve the selected quotation template when adding items.
4. Do not automatically change Keylargo to TESK or another template.
5. Preserve legitimate differences in duty point, quantity, pricing and configuration when users select different values.
6. Do not overwrite existing quotation items when adding new items.
7. Preserve correct brand/model identity after adding multiple items.
8. Ensure the same identity rules apply to Individual, Assembly, Pumpset, System and Manual quotation workflows.

### Important

The investigation showed a difference between 150 m³/h at 30 m and 150 m³/h at 36 m.

Do not automatically classify this as a hydraulic bug.

Determine whether the difference arises from separate user-selected duty points before making any hydraulic calculation changes.
