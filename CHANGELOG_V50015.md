# KeySuite V5.00.15

Phase 2 improvement build based on V5.00.14.

- Added owner-only, 5-second-unlock Category archival with server-side customer dependency protection.
- Unified Company & Pricing with the 13 Pricelist product groups; CR and the four previously missing groups now load their own rules.
- Corrected Customer Brand Margin ordering and suppressed invalid empty-brand entries without deleting stored brands.
- Rebuilt ISO Product presentation on the ES four-filter, series/model and compact-action structure.
- Added a dedicated ISO Product Curve frame and integrated ISO Selection/Product Curve into the V4.28.72 responsive selector container.
- Separated authentication timeouts from secure-data timeouts, added safe session refresh/retry, retained failed-save drafts, and required database read-back confirmation.
- Renamed same-quotation Copy to Clone and added account-scoped Copy/Paste between quotations.
- Preserved quotation numbering/revisions, hydraulic data, pump algorithms and the frozen quotation PDF layout.
